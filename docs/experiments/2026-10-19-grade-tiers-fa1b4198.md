# Grades and looser map tiers of the frozen release (E1c / E24 read-out)

- **Status.** A read-only description of the frozen release's models, run
  2026-10-19. No fit, no refit, no write to the store. Numbers only. It
  serves plan-map-tiers (evidence 3, "Real models") and the grade breakdown
  E1c reports. Read out by the rebuild lane; registered 2026-10-21.
- **Release.** 20261013T191324Z-fa1b4198 (the frozen release).
- **Code.** `grade_tiers.R`, a read-only script outside the Atlas package
  (copy beside this entry, `2026-10-19-grade-tiers-fa1b4198.R`, sha256
  17444e8400045cc123b174e1d5ac2bbcc49635c2827043c92c06a3d8b11a2343). It ran
  in the Atlas image 8bfc98d on the operations box, in about 10 minutes, and
  exited 0.
- **Inputs.** The release JSON, its `occurrences/taxa-latest.json` (each
  taxon's survey sites), and every model's metrics JSON. Held-out Boyce is
  the metrics JSON's `boyce` (pooled), not the exported models table, so the
  table's export fault (fixed on Atlas fix/private-models-boyce) does not
  touch these numbers.
- **Outputs.** Two CSVs on the operations box
  (atlas-box:/home/ubuntu/seamcheck/gt/grade-tiers-models.csv, one row per
  model; grade-tiers-taxa.csv, one row per taxon). They hold taxon names and
  numbers only, and are not published.
- **Rules.** Grades as production: strong = shifted-null AUC p ≤ 0.05 and
  pooled held-out Boyce > 0; weak = not strong, held-out AUC > 0.5 and
  Boyce > 0; failed otherwise. Tiers as plan-map-tiers: moderate = not
  strong, shift p ≤ 0.20 with 25 or more nulls scored, Boyce > 0, AUC > 0.5;
  indicative = not strong or moderate, AUC > 0.5 and Boyce > 0. Moderate-z
  (a variant) = not strong, z = (AUC − null mean) / null SD ≥ 1 and
  Boyce > 0.
- **Cost.** About 10 minutes on the operations box; no worker instance.
