Counts only: no taxon names, ids, places or coordinates.

- **Question.** Which of the frozen pull's records the next models version's
  collection cells must leave out, as the sources stood when the version was
  made, and how many that is beyond release fa1b4198's own privacy list. Serves
  4.1 and S11: the version's cells file and its 3 extra records.
- **Data.** `reproducibility: frozen`. Release 20261013T191324Z-fa1b4198. Its
  pull occurrences-20261012T051017Z-x73b85b36 (sha256 7d3c5d70…, 133,235
  records, pull fingerprint 5e416be5…). Its privacy list privacy-latest.tsv.gz
  of 2026-10-13 03:29 UTC (sha256 e918824d…, 5,475 entries, 2,769 in the pull).
  Its published cells file cells.tsv.gz (sha256 e1d61995…). .org's
  iNaturalist and Mushroom Observer answers as they stood at 2026-11-08 03:13
  UTC, read-only. No layers are read.
- **Code.** `atlas cells-now --out=DIR` (R/cellsnow.R) in image
  ghcr.io/biodiverselabs/mycomap-atlas:ffe5189 (digest sha256:6302ee8c…, merge
  of 05b21e58), on the Atlas box, after a precheck (cells_now_precheck.R,
  sha256 485dfd59…). The launch file is sha256 1a70a159…. A rerun asks .org
  again, so it gives the sources as they stand on the day it runs, not 8
  November.
- **Rule.** A record leaves the cells if any of these holds:
  - (a) it is on the release's privacy list;
  - (b) it is an iNaturalist record that .org's cache now shows obscured, or
    with a positional accuracy coarser than 1 km (over 1,000 m; exactly 1,000 m
    is kept), or that has no cached answer now (a record with no recorded
    accuracy is kept);
  - (c) it is a Mushroom Observer record whose point the observer does not
    show, or that has no cached answer now;
  - (d) it is a Mushroom Observer record the source no longer holds. .org marks
    deletions for Mushroom Observer only.

  Records from other sources (MyCoPortal, DNA sequence records, GenBank) are not
  checked, and are counted where recorded.
